<p align="center"><sub>Every video on our channel is made entirely in Arrumo. People only reviewed and approved each step.</sub></p>

Arrumo works like an editor on your team rather than a clip generator. It drafts each stage, from the script and animations to the previews, the final render, and the titles, descriptions and thumbnails. It shows you the work and waits for your decision before moving on.

- **Your agent, on your plan.** Claude Code or Codex does the creative work, signed in on your own computer.
- **Your brand, learned once.** Point it at your website or channel, and every video follows your context, colors, fonts and logos.
- **Real deliverables.** Every format, subtitles and platform copy, checked against your rules, scheduled and exported together.
- **Local and private.** Your footage, assets and keys stay on your computer.
- **Built to be extended.** Every provider is a plugin, and agents can drive Arrumo through its CLI, MCP and A2A interfaces.
